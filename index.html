THE RULES OF COURTESY

Win the points.
Give away the money.

Three pots. Twenty-four counters. First to 25 points wins.

1

Start

Put one counter into an empty pot. It belongs to you.

2

Take

Add one more than the pot holds to an opponent’s pot. All its counters stay. Now it’s yours.

3

Collect

Empty a pot you own. You score its value. Every counter goes to your opponent’s hand.
